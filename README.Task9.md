# Task 9: Fleet vs Public User Behaviour Analysis

## Project Overview
This task analyses EV charging behaviour across different customer segments, with a particular focus on Fleet and Public users. The objective is to compare charging frequency, energy consumption, customer spending, and revenue contribution to identify potential business opportunities.

## Objectives
- Segment EV charging usage by customer type.
- Compare charging frequency between Fleet and Public users.
- Analyse energy consumption and customer spending.
- Calculate revenue contribution by user type.
- Identify behavioural patterns and recommend fleet partnership strategies.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

### Fleet vs Public Comparison
- Fleet recorded 73,696 charging sessions.
- Public users recorded 73,754 charging sessions.
- Fleet consumed approximately 3.47 million kWh of energy.
- Public users consumed approximately 3.46 million kWh.
- Fleet generated approximately ₹20.52 lakh in revenue.
- Public users generated approximately ₹20.51 lakh.

### Revenue Contribution
- Fleet: 25.11%
- Public: 25.10%
- Delivery: 24.92%
- Taxi: 24.88%

### Behavioural Insights
Fleet and Public users demonstrate very similar charging behaviour. Fleet users show slightly higher average energy consumption and spending, while Public users recorded marginally more sessions.

The differences are small, suggesting that both segments contribute almost equally to EV charging demand and revenue.

## Business Recommendations
1. Develop partnerships with corporate fleet operators.
2. Introduce bulk charging discounts.
3. Offer monthly subscription packages.
4. Explore priority charging access for fleet customers.
5. Monitor fleet charging patterns to optimise station utilisation.

The analysis shows that Fleet and Public customers contribute almost equally to EV charging demand and revenue. EV charging providers can explore fleet partnerships and targeted subscription plans to strengthen customer retention and generate recurring revenue.
